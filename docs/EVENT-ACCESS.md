# Event accessibility: host checklist

This is an implementation and verification checklist, not a claim that all arrangements already exist.

## Confirmed by the organizer

On November 15, 2026, the organizer confirmed that Starr Hill in Scott’s Addition, Basic City Beer in Richmond Southside, and the puzzle event are wheelchair accessible. The three current event pages reflect that confirmation. Specific restroom features, paths to particular event spaces, table clearances, and communication arrangements have not been independently audited.

## Deaf and hard-of-hearing participation

Before each event, agree on and verify:

- Written competition rules available before the start, including how to signal completion.
- A visible start signal synchronized with the spoken start.
- A countdown visible from all participating tables, or an equivalent visual timing arrangement.
- Visual time warnings and a clear visual finish/stop signal.
- Written copies of changes or announcements made over a microphone.
- Hosts who face participants and keep their mouths visible when speaking.
- A text-based way to ask questions before and during the event.
- A plan for requested communication support; do not assume captions or an interpreter are interchangeable for everyone.
- Information on background music/noise, lighting, seating, and any assistive listening equipment.

Do not mark these confirmed in the publishing form until the host can deliver them. Never infer assistive listening equipment from a wheelchair-accessible venue.

## Wheelchair and other mobility access

Confirm the exact event room, entry route, any elevator required, accessible restroom, table approach and clearance, seating space, and emergency exit arrangements. Ask about practical needs without requiring a diagnosis. Avoid making attendees move through blocked aisles or carry equipment to access the puzzle.

## Blind and low-vision participation

Accessible registration and written information do not make a visual puzzle competition equally usable by themselves. Discuss requested participation arrangements with the attendee and explain competition rules and constraints clearly. Provide rules in accessible text, describe relevant visual announcements when needed, and consider seating/lighting needs.

## Publishing policy

All essential event information must be in HTML text rather than only a poster or social image. Describe photos accurately. Review captions for every future video, including speaker identification and meaningful sounds. Provide transcripts for audio. No autoplay audio. No phone-only access request process. The current site contains no audio or video.

## Manual acceptance tests before release

- Keyboard: skip link, all navigation, FAQ disclosures, registration links, photo opening, Escape, and restored focus.
- Screen readers: VoiceOver/Safari and NVDA/Firefox or Chrome; check names, headings, descriptions, reading order, and dialog behavior.
- Reflow: 320 CSS pixels and 200–400% zoom; no horizontal page scrolling or clipped controls.
- Touch: real iPhone and Android; menu, event details, photos, and zoom.
- Reduced motion and forced colors/high contrast.
- Ticket Tailor: reach checkout by keyboard, inspect form labels/errors, verify ticket selection and purchase path in a safe test session. Do not make a real purchase as a test.
- Deaf reviewer/participant feedback on whether event information answers practical questions.

The original `timer.html` remains unchanged; its presence is not confirmation that it will be visible or usable from every table.
